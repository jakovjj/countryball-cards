Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026
Quick Summary: We collect minimal data to improve your experience. If you create a player-community account, we store the account details and content needed to run the Countryball Council, polls, and card reviews. We don't sell your data or share it unnecessarily.

1. Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

  • IP Address & Location: We use your IP address to detect your country and show you a personalized background image. This is done via ipapi.co service.
  • Website Analytics (GA4 & Microsoft Clarity): We measure page views, referral sources (e.g., google.com, reddit.com), basic engagement (e.g., button clicks), device/browser info, and approximate location. Google Analytics 4 is configured with IP anonymization and without Google Signals. Microsoft Clarity records anonymized session replays and heatmaps to help us improve the site experience.
  • Cookies: We use essential first-party cookies to keep Countryball Council members signed in for up to 30 days, plus analytics cookies to understand site usage. Council login cookies contain random authentication values rather than your Google password or personal details, and are removed when you log out. Some third parties, such as Google Analytics, may set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Device Information: Basic browser and device information for site optimization.

Information You Provide

  • Email Address: Only collected if you voluntarily sign up for our email updates via the Tally form.
  • Community Account: If you sign in to the player community, Google provides your account identifier, email address, name, profile image, and email-verification status. We use that information to authenticate you. Our Omnifill order system checks the same email for a Countryball Cards purchase and returns the matching game-edition SKU. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Community Content: Council posts, attached images, poll votes, and card reviews you submit are stored so the community features work. Your display name, game-edition tag, posts, attached images, and reviews are visible to other signed-in Council members; your email address and individual poll choices are not public.
  • Discord/Reddit Interactions: If you join our Discord or Reddit communities, those platforms have their own privacy policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalization: Show you country-specific background images based on your location
  • Communication: Send you game updates if you subscribe to our email list
  • Community: Authenticate verified players, verify game ownership, assign edition tags, operate discussions and polls, publish card reviews, prevent abuse, and moderate the community
  • Analytics: Understand website traffic and improve user experience
  • Legal Compliance: Meet any applicable legal requirements

3. Information Sharing

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with:

  • Service Providers:
    • ipapi.co for country detection
    • Google for account sign-in and analytics via Google Identity Services and Google Analytics 4
    • Microsoft for session recording via Microsoft Clarity
    • Tally.so for email form handling
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights
