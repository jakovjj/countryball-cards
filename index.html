About Countryball Cards

Countryball Cards is an indie strategy card game inspired by countryball memes and tabletop tactics. Build a team of nations, combine unique abilities, and play with friends. We publish regular balance updates and new cards based on community testing.

Try the print‑and‑play

Join our Discord to get the free print‑and‑play PDF and help us tune abilities before the Kickstarter launch. You can also follow updates by email using the button above.

Game Features

Learn how to play our strategic card game featuring resource management, tactical combat, and unique countryball abilities. Use our online dice roller for attacks and market prices. Choose from multiple game packages (editions) when we launch.
