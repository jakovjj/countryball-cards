What's Countryball Cards?

Countryball Cards is a tabletop card game in which you draft your very own team of countryballs! Build your economy. Unleash ridiculous combos. Dominate the tabletop.
  • Draft a squad that clicks: pick countryballs that combo together, or that hard-counter what your opponents are building.
  • Win with brains, not luck: smart decisions with coins + resources decide who snowballs and who gets rolled.
  • Huge replay value: 40+ unique abilities across 20 unique countries. (Even more with the Extended Edition)
  • 1v1 and 2v2 battles: fast turns, big swings, and just enough chaos to keep every game fresh.

Crash Course

1. Setup

Draft countryball teams (3 Countryball Cards each). Set the market with resource dice, then decide who starts.

2. Your Turn

Gain coins (10x first turn, 5x for the rest of the game), buy/sell resources, use abilities (pay costs), then attack enemies.

3. Abilities

Some abilities require a die roll. Meet the number shown to succeed; attacks deal damage and can stun.

4. Win

Eliminate enemy countryballs by dealing damage (each Countryball has 3 hitpoints). Last player standing wins.
