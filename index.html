Quick answers on gameplay, what's in the box, and how fulfillment works for Countryball Cards.

    Yes. You can request a refund any time before we ship. If you need to change your order details, email us and we’ll help. We answer all queries within 24 hours.

  • Do you ship internationally?

    We ship to 52 countries. Available destinations and prices show at checkout.

  • How is shipping calculated?

    Shipping is calculated based on destination during checkout so you can see the total before paying.

    As we share on Kickstarter, fulfillment can take a little time because production and freight happen in stages. We’re already in production and will ship as soon as everything clears.
